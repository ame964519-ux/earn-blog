## The 4-Stage Product Creation & Launch Workflow

```
[Stage 1: Wireframing] --> [Stage 2: Design & Links] --> [Stage 3: 3D Mockups] --> [Stage 4: SEO Listing]
  Figma / Paper Sketch       Canva Pro / Keynote / Notion   Placeit & Canva 3D        Etsy 13 Tags & Video
  Plan navigation layout     Add functional hyperlinks       Photorealistic iPad art   Target buyer keywords
```

### 1. Planning & Wireframing the System
Before designing, map out every page and database connection:
- For a **Digital Planner**: Index page, Year at a Glance, 12 Monthly spreads, 52 Weekly spreads, and 365 Daily pages, with dedicated tabs for Fitness, Finances, and Meal Planning.
- For a **Notion Template**: Build interconnected databases (e.g., Projects linked to Tasks, Clients linked to Invoices) using Notion Formulas 2.0.

### 2. Design & Hyperlinking in Canva Pro or Apple Keynote
- Use **Apple Keynote** or **Canva Pro** to design master slide layouts with tabs on the right side and top navigation bars.
- Link every tab to its corresponding page in the document.
- Export as **PDF with Interactive Links** (ensure PDF compression keeps file sizes under 25MB for seamless iPad loading).

### 3. Creating Scroll-Stopping 3D Mockups
In digital product sales, **the listing mockup IS your product packaging**:
- High-converting Etsy listings feature an iPad Pro resting on a clean aesthetic desk with coffee, stationery, and a stylus.
- Use tools like **Canva Mockups, Placeit.net, or Figma** to render your planner pages inside photorealistic device frames.
- Include an 8-second video preview showing someone flicking through the hyperlinked tabs on an iPad screen.

### 4. Etsy SEO & Keyword Tag Optimization
Rank on the first page of Etsy search by optimizing the 3 core algorithmic factors:
- **Product Title**: Include front-loaded high-intent search terms (e.g., *"2026 Digital Planner for GoodNotes | Hyperlinked iPad Daily Weekly Monthly Planner | Minimalist Aesthetic Life OS"*).
- **13 Etsy Tags**: Use all 13 multi-word long-tail tags (e.g., `ipad planner`, `goodnotes template`, `dated daily planner`, `notability planner`, `digital life os`).
- **Detailed Description**: Provide clear instructions on how downloads work, compatible apps, and a bulleted feature breakdown.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Do I need advanced graphic design skills or a Mac to create digital planners?
No. Free design platforms like **Canva** and free presentation tools like **Google Slides or Canva Pro** provide intuitive drag-and-drop editors for creating hyperlinked PDF planners on any Windows PC or Mac.

### How do customers receive their digital planner after purchasing?
When a customer completes checkout on Etsy or Gumroad, the platform immediately delivers a secure instant download link containing the hyperlinked PDF or a "Click here to duplicate Notion template" link, accompanied by a 1-page PDF quick-start guide.

### Can I legally sell digital templates made in Canva?
Yes. Canva's commercial license allows you to sell unique template designs you created from scratch using Canva elements, provided you sell them as finished end-products (e.g., flattened hyperlinked PDFs or Canva template links) rather than standalone raw stock graphics.
