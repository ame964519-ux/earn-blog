## Executive Summary

How B2B lead generation agencies set up secondary domains, email warmups, and personalized AI prompts to book qualified sales calls for tech companies.

## 1. Setting Up Secondary Email Infrastructure

Never send cold campaigns from your primary domain. Set up 3 to 5 auxiliary domains with proper SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records to ensure 99% inbox placement.

## 2. AI-Driven List Building & Personalization

Extract verified target decision-maker leads using Apollo or Clay, then use AI to write relevant, non-spammy opening lines referencing recent company milestones.

## 3. Pay-Per-Appointment Pricing Model

Charge clients $250 to $500 for every qualified decision-maker that attends a sales discovery call, providing a frictionless risk-free pitch.

## Strategic Action Steps to Implement This Model

1. **Commit to one monetization vertical**: Focus entirely on executing this single blueprint for at least 30 continuous days.
2. **Build your automated distribution asset**: Set up your blog, storefront, or workflow using 100% free cloud tiers.
3. **Analyze performance and scale**: Review your conversion data weekly to optimize traffic funnels and multiply your earnings.
