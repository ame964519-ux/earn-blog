## 2. Mastering Audio Engineering Standards

Use free audio tools like Audacity to master tracks to ACX (Audible) technical specifications: -3dB peak levels, -23dB to -18dB RMS volume, and sub-60dB background noise floors.

## 3. Packaging Narration Packages for Independent Authors
