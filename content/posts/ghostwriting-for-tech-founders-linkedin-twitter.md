## Executive Summary

A practical framework for ghostwriting insightful thought leadership posts on LinkedIn and X for busy startup executives and venture capital partners.

## 1. The High Demand for Executive Personal Branding

CEOs and founders need active digital presences to recruit top engineering talent, close enterprise enterprise deals, and attract investors, but lack the time to draft daily content.

## 2. Conducting a 30-Minute Weekly Voice Interview

Interview your client for 30 minutes recording key opinions and lessons. Transcribe the audio and use AI structuring templates to produce a full week of high-impact posts.

## 3. Retainer Pricing & Client Retention

Charge a flat retainer of $1,500 to $3,000 per month for 3 to 4 weekly LinkedIn posts plus bi-weekly long-form newsletter articles.

## Strategic Action Steps to Get Started

1. **Focus on one clear monetization angle**: Commit to mastering this specific channel for at least 30 uninterrupted days.
2. **Build your digital distribution pipeline**: Set up daily output habits and track audience response metrics systematically.
3. **Analyze and double down**: Use real performance analytics to reinvest in what generates the highest conversion rates.
