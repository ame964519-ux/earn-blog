## Executive Summary

A step-by-step master breakdown on creating viral 15-second product review clips and earning automated commission payouts directly through TikTok Shop.

## 1. The TikTok Shop Commission Revolution

Unlike traditional affiliate marketing where users must click a link in bio, TikTok Shop features in-video shopping tags. Viewers can purchase with one tap, leading to conversion rates 5x higher than standard affiliate links.

## 2. Finding High-Commission Viral Products

Use the TikTok Shop Creator Marketplace to filter products offering 15% to 30% commissions with proven sales velocity. Focus on portable gadgets, aesthetic room decor, and trending beauty accessories.

## 3. The 3-Part Viral Video Formula

Hook in the first 2 seconds highlighting a common pain point, showcase the product solving it within 7 seconds, and end with an urgent call-to-action directing viewers to the orange shopping cart button.
