## Executive Summary

Step-by-step masterclass on finding crawl errors, Core Web Vitals bottlenecks, and indexing issues for businesses with high willingness to pay.

## 1. What is a Technical SEO Audit?

A technical audit analyzes site speed, mobile responsiveness, XML sitemaps, structured data schema, and canonical tags to remove ranking roadblocks on Google.

## 2. Free Auditing Tools You Can Use Today

Leverage Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, Screaming Frog (free version), and Ahrefs Webmaster Tools to identify critical site issues.

## 3. Delivering Actionable Video Walkthroughs

Record a 5-minute Loom video walking the client through 3 urgent fixes. This builds instant trust and converts one-off audits into $1,000/month ongoing maintenance retainers.
