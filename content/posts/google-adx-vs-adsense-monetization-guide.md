## Executive Summary

Learn when and how to transition from Google AdSense to Google AdX (Ad Exchange) and programmatic header bidding to increase your page RPMs by 50% to 150%.

## 1. What is Google AdX?

While AdSense connects your site to the Google Display Network, Google Ad Exchange (AdX) invites hundreds of programmatic demand partners to bid in real-time auctions for every single ad impression.

## 2. The Magic of Programmatic Header Bidding

Header bidding runs simultaneous auctions in user browsers before page rendering, forcing ad networks to compete aggressively and driving up effective CPM prices.

## 3. Qualification Requirements and Channel Partners

Websites with over 30,000 to 50,000 monthly page views can access Google AdX through certified publishing partners like Ezoic, Mediavine, or Monumetric without meeting direct enterprise minimums.
