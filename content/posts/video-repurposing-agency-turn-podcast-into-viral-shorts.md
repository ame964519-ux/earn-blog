## 4-Step Workflow: From Raw Podcast to 30 Polished Viral Clips

### Step 1: Automated Virality Detection with Opus Clip
1. Copy the YouTube link of the client's latest podcast episode and paste it into **Opus Clip**.
2. The AI algorithm analyzes facial expressions, voice pitch modulation, storytelling peaks, and topic hooks, generating 10 to 15 vertical clips ranked by a **Virality Score (0–100)**.
3. Export the top 6 highest-scoring clips in 1080x1920 vertical format.

### Step 2: High-Retention Kinetic Captions in CapCut
Import the clips into **CapCut Desktop**:
- Generate **Auto-Captions** with 1 click.
- Choose high-contrast font styles (e.g. *The Bold Font* or *Montserrat Black*) with yellow/green keyword highlights.
- Keep only 2 to 4 words per line on screen to maximize viewer retention and average watch time.

### Step 3: Insert Visual Pattern Interrupts
Social media algorithms reward retention. Add visual switches every 3 to 4 seconds:
- **Fast 1.15x Zoom-in** on key punchlines.
- **Relevant B-Roll Overlay** (e.g. when the host mentions *"Bitcoin"* or *"real estate"*).
- **Subtle Whoosh / Pop Sound Effects** on animated icons.

### Step 4: Batch Delivery & Retainer Setup
Export your 30 monthly clips (1 clip per day for the client) and upload them to a shared Google Drive or Frame.io workspace.
